Coffee Roastery · Baku · Brand identity concept

4 letters.4 brews.1 line.

Four ways to make coffee are hidden in MOKA's logo: the moka pot, the cup, the Chemex and the V60. The line cutting through the letters is the level of coffee in the cup. The whole identity is built on that line.

Explore the conceptAR demo

Concept

Every letter is a brewing tool

Moka pot. A trapezoid body with a notched lid. Strong, dense stovetop coffee; the classic home brewer.

Cup. A cup seen from above, and a coffee bean at the same time. The symbol of the espresso bar.

Chemex. An hourglass shape with a handle. A clean, clear cup; it is also the icon on the cups.

V60. A cone dripper. The pour-over method that shows a bean's tasting notes most clearly.

The roast line

The height of the line shows the roast

Light roasts carry the line high, dark roasts low. Bags standing side by side on the shelf become a scale: customers choose without reading. The flavour colour shows what you will taste.

In the café

The line becomes light

On the main wall, at the height of the signature line, a warm LED strip runs through the large letters, the counter and the shelves. The interior already has LED lines; raising one of them to this height creates the café's photo spot.

The line ritual

Every cup exactly to the line

Dine-in cups have a fine line inside, and the barista fills the coffee right up to it. A small detail, but every guest sees it and remembers it. The “Hit the line” game is the digital version of this ritual.
