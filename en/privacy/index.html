Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

This site is a concept / test project for MOKA Roastery & Eatery. There is no server side, no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Stored on your device

Only these are kept in your browser storage (localStorage): your language, your best game scores, your MOKA Club points and codes, and whether the intro animation was shown. Nothing is sent anywhere; you can delete it with the button on the Club page.

Camera

The AR pages open the camera only after you allow it. The image is processed on your device, in the browser; no photo or video is uploaded. Only you save or share your selfie.

Third-party services

The AR libraries and fonts load from this site itself. Only the coffee selfie downloads Google MediaPipe's face-tracking model (from jsDelivr and googleapis); those services may see standard technical data (IP address, browser). The site is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Cookies

The site does not use cookies.
