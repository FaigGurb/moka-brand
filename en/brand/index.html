Foundation

MOKA’s official brand book

This book has two layers. The foundation is MOKA’s official brand book: the logo, the black-and-white palette, the typefaces, the slogans and the packaging. It does not change. The system is the idea hidden inside that foundation: four letters, four brews, one line. The following sections are the rules of the system.

  • 01 · MOKA’s official brand bookFoundationLogo and “Roastery & Eatery” · black-and-white palette · Yume and Avenir · slogans · packaging
  • 02 · This bookSystem4 letters, 4 brews, 1 line · day colours · tone of voice · motion and sound · AR and games

Platform

Proposal

Purpose

To brew coffee with care in Baku and make that care visible in every cup.

Positioning

A Baku roastery that takes coffee seriously but speaks simply: it shows the process instead of hiding it.

Brand idea

4 letters · 4 brews · 1 line

Values

  • PrecisionThe line is not an accident: grams, minutes, degrees. Every cup to the same level.
  • OpennessWe show where the bean comes from, how it is roasted and brewed.
  • WarmthOak, copper, morning light. From Baku, sincere, unhurried.
  • PlayLetters are tools, the line is a level — the brand invites discovery.

Who it is for

Hypotheses — to be checked with real customer interviews.

  • The morning rush

    A takeaway cup on the way to work. Wants speed and a precise taste.

  • The brewing enthusiast

    V60, Chemex, origin. Looks for detail and story.

  • The meeting place

    Friends, a laptop, a long stay. Space and atmosphere matter.

Logo

Each letter is a brewer; the gap that cuts through the letters is the coffee level in the cup — the signature line.

Clear space

½ H

Keep free space around the logo of at least half the letter height. No text, edge or other logo enters this zone.

Minimum size

The line gap is 0.74% of the logo width. Below 1 pixel the line disappears, so:

Grid and spacing

The site uses a 1320 px maximum width with 20/40 px margins. Spacing follows multiples of 8. The signature line is a layout divider too: 3 px, full width.

  • Max width 1320 px
  • Margins: 20 px phone, 40 px desktop
  • Spacing: 4 · 8 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64 · 96
  • Divider: the 3 px signature line
  • Corners: square (round only for avatars and swatches)

Photography

Photos are built on the line too: there is always one horizontal line in the frame — a table edge, the coffee level, a shelf. It sits in the lower third.

  • LightWarm window light from the side. No flash.
  • LineA horizontal line in the lower third: table, shelf, coffee level.
  • ProcessHands and moments: pouring, grinding, roasting. Process over result.
  • MaterialOak, copper, concrete, paper. No plastic, no busy backgrounds.
  • ColourNatural colour, no filters. Let the day colours appear by themselves.

For baristas: 6 steps with a phone

  1. 1Wipe the lens.
  2. 2Stand near a window, take the light from the side.
  3. 3Turn on the grid; put the line in the lower third.
  4. 4Tap the cup on screen to focus.
  5. 5Pull exposure down a little — colours stay rich.
  6. 6Shoot five frames, not one; pick the simplest.

We avoid

  • ✕Stock photos
  • ✕Heavy filters and vignettes
  • ✕Tilted horizons
  • ✕Photographing people without consent

Tone of voice

Proposal

MOKA speaks like a barista: knows, but doesn't lecture; warm, but never sugary.

  • Simple

    Short sentences, everyday words.

    We sayOn the bar today: Ethiopia, light roast.

    We don't sayHurry to discover a unique coffee experience!

  • Precise

    Numbers and facts, no hype.

    We say15 g coffee, 250 g water, 3 minutes.

    We don't sayThe tastiest coffee in the world, guaranteed!

  • Warm

    Sincere, local, unhurried.

    We sayCome in, sit down. Coffee's ready.

    We don't sayDear customers, please be informed that…

  • Playful

    We play with letters and the line, in moderation.

    We sayK = Chemex. Today we fill to the line.

    We don't sayCofffeeee 🔥🔥🔥 at MOKA!!!

Tagline

To the line.

  • CupThis cup goes to the line.
  • BagThe line shows the roast.
  • ClubCollect points, reach the line.
  • CaféEvery cup right up to the line.

Naming

  • Drinks keep their international names: Espresso, Flat white, Cold brew.
  • Seasonal series carry the name of a day colour: Səhər series, Axşam series.
  • Limited releases: Nar № [0001] — with the artist's name.

Writing rules

  • Azerbaijani letters always correct: ə, ğ, ı, ö, ş, ü, ç — never replaced by “e”.
  • Price: 4.50 ₼ · weight: 250 g · temperature: 93 °C.
  • Sentence case; CAPITALS only in design labels.
  • At most one emoji per post; none on the menu.
  • The three languages carry the same meaning, not a word-for-word translation.

Motion

Every movement comes from coffee itself: it pours, settles and reaches the line. Motion never hides content and switches off with “reduce motion”.

  • Pour

    Starts slow and speeds up (ease-in). Intros, reveals.

  • Settle

    Arrives fast and stops softly (ease-out). Cards, text.

  • Line

    Draws at a steady speed (linear). Progress, dividers, timers.
